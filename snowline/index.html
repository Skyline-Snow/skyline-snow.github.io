Forecasts from Open-Meteo: NOAA GFS and NBM, ECMWF IFS, Environment Canada GEM, DWD ICON. Snowfall is worked out from each model's precipitation and the temperature at that elevation. With local tuning on, the snow-to-liquid ratio comes from measured snow near Bachelor (see "How this forecast is tuned"); with it off, a generic ratio of about 8:1 near freezing up to 16:1 in very cold air is used. Mid and summit wind is free-air wind interpolated between the 850 and 700 hPa levels; base wind is the model's 10 m wind. Beyond about day 7 forecasts lose most of their skill, so those days are hatched. Use the ensemble range to judge them.
